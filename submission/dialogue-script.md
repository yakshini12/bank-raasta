# Bank Raasta demo dialogue

**Format:** a ~35 s motion-graphics teaser (brand card, the problem shown through pictures with burned-in subtitle pills, RBI scale bridge) followed by the working-prototype walkthrough and a closing card. English (India) narration; the audio is dialogue only — no music or ambience noise — with every line placed on the scene beat it describes. Delivery uses an expressive Indian-English voice with per-line pitch and rate variation so the read does not sound mechanical. The voices are generated narration, separate from the Gnani voice features in the app. Stock photos and drawn mock screens are illustrative and do not depict the speakers or real cases.
